The Story Behind the Domain

Why garabed.org?

A personal website I first built in 2001. Over the decades it has taken on many iterations — each one a reflection of where I was in life, what I was learning, and where I was headed. It was never just a website. It was the jump point.

2001

The Beginning

Garabed.org started as a personal website — hand-coded HTML, in the early days of broadband internet, and a lot of curiosity. It was my corner of the early web, a place to experiment and build something from nothing.

2003

The Jump Into IT

The site became my launchpad. Maintaining it pushed me deep into network infrastructure — routing, switching, LAN/WAN architecture, and the systems that keep data moving reliably. It also sparked a hands-on interest in mass storage: RAID arrays, SAN and NAS configurations, and the discipline of keeping data available and protected. What started as a hobby quickly became a professional foundation.

2005

Programming & Storage

Iterations of the site brought deeper dives into scripting and programming — automating tasks, building dynamic pages, and exploring storage systems. This era included the development of a custom search engine and an internal email messaging platform, both built from the ground up. Each version of garabed.org reflected what I was learning at the time.

2008

Security

Running a public-facing domain for years taught me hard lessons about security. I studied it, applied it, and eventually made it a core competency — spanning VPN architecture, firewall design, IPsec tunneling, and OCC compliance & auditing. Understanding that every system is only as strong as its weakest exposure became a guiding principle.

2010s

Voice Systems

The evolution moved into enterprise voice at scale — leading large corporate migrations of legacy voice systems, designing global call routing and least cost routing strategies that delivered significant cost savings, and executing migrations to Cisco voice platforms and Skype for Business. This era also included deploying and managing web and audio conference hosting solutions for distributed organizations across multiple continents.

Today

A Living Record

Over two decades later, garabed.org is still here. It has changed shape with every chapter of my career, but its purpose remains the same: a personal mark on the internet that represents a lifetime of building, learning, and delivering.

And Garabed.com was already taken. : )
